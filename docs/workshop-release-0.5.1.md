## English change notes

Krypton Survivor 0.5.1

- Added innate heat vision: aim with the mouse and press R. No equipment, hunger, sanity or durability cost.
- The beam pierces multiple enemies and can ignite surviving flammable targets. Damage is independently configurable at 2,000, 10,000 or 50,000. The local key can be changed to G, Z or disabled.
- Fixed beams starting behind the head. They now extend forward from the eyes and follow head movement, with two beams in front views and one in profile. Added a brighter core, glow and eye highlights.
- Retains Rao's Edge, full night vision, and surface daytime health and sanity regeneration.

Direct heat vision hits exclude players, followers, companions, domesticated creatures, walls and structures. Native fire from ignited targets can still spread.

## 可追加到工坊介绍的技能说明

### 热视线

超人自带远程能力，无需护目镜或其他装备。鼠标瞄准世界中的方向，按 R 发射；空手或持有拉奥之锋均可使用。射程 20，无资源消耗，最短发射间隔 0.35 秒。按键和伤害可在 Mod 配置中调整。

光束可以穿透直线上的多个敌人，并点燃仍存活的可燃、非火焰免疫生物。玩家、随从、伙伴、驯化生物、墙和建筑免受直接命中，但点燃后的原版火焰可能蔓延。聊天、界面操作、建筑放置、忙碌、工作、死亡、幽灵和服务器暂停期间无法发射。

### Heat vision

Superman has innate ranged heat vision, with no goggles or other equipment required. Aim into the world with the mouse and press R; it works unarmed or while holding Rao's Edge. Range: 20. No resource cost. Minimum cast interval: 0.35 seconds. The key and damage can be adjusted in the mod configuration.

The beam pierces multiple enemies along a straight line and can ignite surviving flammable creatures that are not fire-immune. Direct hits exclude players, followers, companions, domesticated creatures, walls and structures, but native fire can spread. Casting is blocked while chatting, interacting with UI, placing buildings, busy, working, dead, a ghost, or while the server is paused.
